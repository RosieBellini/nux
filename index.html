NUX Seminar

A weekly seminar on new user experience research at NYU.

About Us

+

NUX is an interdisciplinary seminar at NYU bringing together researchers, students, and practitioners to discuss emerging work in human-computer interaction, user experience, and technology design.

We meet weekly during the semester to read and discuss recent papers, hear from guest speakers, and collaborate on research questions at the intersection of people and technology.

Get Involved

+
  • Attend a session — Sessions are open to all NYU affiliates. Check the Readings page for upcoming topics.
  • Join the Slack — Connect with the community on the NYU HCI Slack ↗.
  • Present your work — Reach out to the hosts if you'd like to share ongoing research.
  • Suggest a reading — We welcome paper suggestions from the community.
