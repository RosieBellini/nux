About Us

NUX is an interdisciplinary seminar at NYU bringing together researchers, students, and practitioners to discuss emerging work in human-computer interaction, user experience, and technology design. We meet weekly during the semester to read and discuss recent papers, hear from guest speakers, and collaborate on research questions at the intersection of people and technology.

Founded at NYU, NUX (New User Experience) brings together people from computer science, psychology, law, design, and social science to look at HCI problems from multiple angles.

Our sessions combine close reading of current papers with open discussion. No background in any particular field is required — curiosity is enough.

  • Attend a session — Open to all NYU affiliates. Check the Readings page for upcoming topics and times.
  • Join the Slack — Connect on the NYU HCI Slack ↗.
  • Present your work — Reach out to the hosts if you'd like to share ongoing research.
  • Suggest a reading — We welcome paper suggestions from the community.
